## 📌 Overview

**`ikshvaku-general-awareness`** is the official **General Awareness & General Studies Question Bank** for the **Ikshvaku Aptitude Arena** by **Ikshvaku Vidya Academy**.

It contains **500,000+ verified questions** modeled on real **SSC CGL & SSC CHSL previous year exam papers (2018–2024 Tier-1 & Tier-2)** across 17 standardized GS subjects.

---

## 🏗️ Supported Topics (17 Major Domains)

1. **Indian Polity & Constitution** (`POLITY`) - Articles, Amendments, Fundamental Rights, Parliament
2. **Ancient & Medieval History** (`HIST`) - Indus Valley, Vedic, Delhi Sultanate, Mughals
3. **Modern History & Freedom Struggle** (`MODHIST`) - 1857 Revolt, INC Sessions, Gandhian Era
4. **Indian Geography** (`GEOG`) - River Systems, Waterfalls, National Parks, Climate & Soils
5. **Physical & World Geography** (`WGEOG`) - Solar system, Lithosphere, Oceans, Continents
6. **Indian Economy & Planning** (`ECON`) - RBI, Monetary Policy, Five-Year Plans, Fiscal Deficit
7. **General Physics** (`PHYS`) - SI Units, Mechanics, Optics, Sound, Electricity
8. **General Chemistry** (`CHEM`) - Chemical formulas, Everyday compounds, Periodic table
9. **General Biology** (`BIO`) - Human body systems, Diseases, Vitamins, Plant cells
10. **Art & Culture** (`CULTURE`) - Classical dances (Kathakali, Bharatanatyam), Festivals, Temples
11. **Books & Authors** (`BOOKS`) - Historical classics, Modern literary prize winners
12. **Awards & Honors** (`AWARDS`) - Bharat Ratna, Padma Awards, Nobel, Oscar
13. **Sports & Tournaments** (`SPORTS`) - Olympics, Cricket World Cups, Asian Games, Trophies
14. **Static GK** (`STATICGK`) - International Headquarters, Dams, Biosphere Reserves
15. **Computer Awareness** (`COMP`) - Hardware, Memory, Shortcuts, Protocols
16. **Environment & Ecology** (`ECOL`) - Ramsar Sites, Wildlife Protection Act, Climate Treaties
17. **Current Affairs & Schemes** (`CA`) - Central Government Schemes & Welfare Initiatives
